Background

As a passionate game designer and developer based in Leiria, Portugal , I graduated a year ago with a degree in Video Games in IPLeiria, complemented by a professional course in Web Development in IPLeiria.
My journey in the industry includes a five-month internship at Nerd Monkeys, where I contributed to two exciting projects using Unity and Unreal Engine 5, deepening my understanding of game mechanics and development processes. In addition to my professional experience, I’ve developed my own projects showcased on Itch.io, including a 2D side-scroller and a 3D point-and-click game, where I took on the roles of game designer and project manager. Furthermore, my GitHub features other websites I've developed, reflecting my commitment to creativity and technical expertise.

I’m currently seeking new opportunities to further expand my skills and contribute to innovative game development.
